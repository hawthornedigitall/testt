What we do

Professional plastering services

From refreshing tired walls to preparing complete rooms, we provide practical plastering solutions for homes and local businesses.

01 / SKIMMING

Skimming

A smooth, even finish for new walls and ceilings, ready for decorating once fully dry.

02 / RESKIMMING

Reskimming

Refresh cracked, uneven or tired surfaces without replacing the entire wall.

03 / PLASTERBOARDING

Plasterboarding

New plasterboard installation for walls, ceilings, extensions and renovation projects.

04 / REPAIRS

Plaster Repairs

Reliable repairs for cracks, holes, damaged sections and areas affected by renovation work.
